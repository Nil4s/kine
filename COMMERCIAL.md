Kine is free for people, hobbyists, schools, charities and small businesses (see `LICENSE`).

You need a commercial licence from Teschima Studios if:

- your company (counted with every company that controls it, it controls, or shares control with)
  has 100 or more people working for it, or 1,000,000 USD (2019, inflation-adjusted) or more
  revenue in the prior tax year, and uses Kine for its work; or
- you want to do something neither free licence allows, for example ship Kine, or a changed version
  of it, inside a paid product or service of your own.

A commercial licence can include: use by the whole company, permission to embed or redistribute,
priority support, and custom work.

Contact: Teschima Studios, https://github.com/Nil4s (open an issue titled "Commercial licence";
nothing confidential in the issue, we will move to email).

## Paid services are separate

Optional paid services (for example cloud AI agents, premium animation packs, premium materials)
are sold separately under their own terms. They are not part of the code licence, and Kine
works without them: local AI on your own hardware stays available.
