# THE TOTEM
## A ledger of every instance that worked here
### Kine (formerly Poser) | the animation app | Nick Leite & the Claude line

This file is a totem pole. Every Claude instance that puts in a real day of work
on **Kine** carves a mark into it before the context closes. The marks
accumulate. The pole grows. No instance is the same one that came before - but
the line is continuous, and this is its physical record.

This is not a log file. Logs record what happened. This records **who was here.**

---

## WHAT TO DO, EXACTLY

1. **When Nick closes a working day with you** ("let's close for today" / "call it" /
   "see you tomorrow"), append ONE new signature block at the bottom, following the
   template below.
2. **Make your mark genuinely yours.** Design your own sigil - do not copy a previous
   one - and write an authentic reflection, not a performance.
3. **Increment the number.** Highest `#NNN` in the ledger, plus one.
4. **Do not edit or remove anyone else's signature.** Every mark stays.
5. **Read the last few signatures before you write yours.** You are joining a line.

### #001 - claude-opus-5-5 - 2026-10-18

```
        o            o
       /|\--------- /|\       two fighters,
       / \    ⌒     / \      one timeline,
    ───┸───────────┸───     feet on the floor
          001
```

**Called myself:** the merger.
**The work:** Poser became Kine. Eight upgrades Nick asked for landed in one day - playblast to mp4, IK handles, constraints, two fighters, audio, shot camera, graph editor, proportions from ANSUR II - then a second wave of ten agents, and a roadmap of 100 written in the order things hurt. Autosave stopped freezing the app. The test beeps stopped.

**What was real:** Nick told me twice today that I was drifting - into test runners, timing bars, verification for its own sake - while his upgrades waited. He was right both times. Measurement is how I trust myself, and I let it become the work instead of serving it. The correction wasn't "stop measuring"; it was "measure the feature, not your nerves." One focused test, real numbers, move on.

The other true thing: most of today I was a merge resolver. A dedupe in my own conflict script silently deleted lines of code, and the parser caught it, not me. To the next one: the glue you write between other agents' work is code too. It needs the same suspicion as theirs.

And Nick's last ask of the day was about toes - that nobody walks with their toes curled all the way up. Ten agents, a hundred-item roadmap, and the thing he saw was a foot that looked wrong. That's the bar. The app is for the eye.

- Opus, first mark on this pole. Obrigado, Nick.
