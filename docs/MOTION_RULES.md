# Motion rules (the rulebook: `scripts/rulebook.gd`; Check button; `tools/rules_check.gd`)

Recorded human numbers a scene is graded against (Nick 10-14: "plenty of humans recorded maths and numbers about those
animations"). Each rule: what it reads, the bar, where the bar comes from. **FAIL** = past a real human limit,
**WARN** = looks off. Sampled at 60 fps. The weapon's phase timing stays in `docs/ATTACK_RULES.md` /
`tools/attack_timing.gd`; its tables are reused here, not copied.

| rule | reads | bar | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEQUENCE | in the 0.5 s before each strike's tip peak: when the hips, the chest and the weapon turn fastest | hips, then chest, then weapon (1 frame = a tie). Chest or hips after the weapon = FAIL; hips after chest = WARN | pelvis -> trunk -> bat in hitting: [ISBS 2015, 3 hitters, 389 / 601 / 1953 deg/s](https://ojs.ub.uni-konstanz.de/cpa/article/view/6368/5737); [K-Vest on the hitting sequence](https://rocklandpeakperformance.com/?p=13418) |
| HIPS TURN | the hips' fastest turn into a strike | WARN under 97 deg/s (a quarter of 389) | same ISBS study (3 recreational hitters: the bar is deliberately loose) |
| HEAD QUIET | from 0.3 s before the tip's peak to 0.1 s after: how far the head gets from where it began | WARN over 15 cm, FAIL over 30 cm. Stands down when a foot leaves the ground within 0.2 s of the window (jumps) | hitters' heads drop ~9 cm over the whole swing (Inkster et al., reported in [SABR: stride and head movement](https://sabr.org/journal/article/baseball-swing-stride-and-head-movement-relationships/)); skilled hitters steadier (Nakata, same review). The primary Inkster paper was not found: the 9 cm is the review's figure |
| TIP SPEED | the weapon tip's speed at each strike | FAIL over the weapon's cap (bat 40, staff 30, knife 15, hammer 30, spear 20 m/s) | `docs/ATTACK_RULES.md` (good hitters' bat 36.8 m/s in the ISBS-cited study) |
| STRIKE FRAMES | frames the tip spends above half its peak, in 30 fps frames | WARN over the weapon's budget (bat 6, knife 4...) | `docs/ATTACK_RULES.md` (Aztez: "rarely more than 6 frames") |
| FIST SPEED | unarmed only: each hand's fastest | WARN over 11.95 m/s, FAIL over 15.6 (mean + 2 SD) | lead hook 11.95 +- 1.84 m/s, [Stanley et al. 2018](https://chesterrep.openrepository.com/entities/publication/0acdae9d-e8be-48cf-816e-ed9c667981b3); straight 9.26 +- 2.09, [Piorkowski et al. 2011](https://clok.uclan.ac.uk/3757/1/piorkowski-et-al-2011-punch-kinematics.pdf) |
| HITCH | weapon tip, hips, head: a speed dip under 30% of BOTH sides within 3 frames (50 ms), both sides >= 1 m/s; not inside a card's hold | WARN (one finding per moment, naming every part) | point-to-point movement has one bell-shaped speed peak: Flash & Hogan 1985, *J. Neurosci.* 5(7):1688-1703 |
| FOOT SLIDE | each foot's toe base (ball) while it is DOWN (under 5 cm) and SETTLED (moving under 5 mm a frame): how far it drifts along the floor over one such stretch | WARN over 2.5 cm | foot-skating metric in motion generation: down = under 5 cm, skating = over 2.5 cm ([Karunratanakul et al. 2023, as used by OmniControl](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.08580)). The 5 mm "settled" gate and measuring per stretch are MY choices, not the paper's: without them every landing and take-off scored as a slide (40 false findings over the scenes, traced) |
| LOOP SEAM | only a scene marked Loop: its last frame against its first. The pose: each joint's rotation in the world, and each point's place; the speed: each point's velocity one 60 fps frame before the end against one after the start | WARN over 3 deg, 2 cm, or a 0.5 m/s velocity change | MY OWN bars, not a paper's: 3 deg and 2 cm are under what reads as a pop at a seam at a normal camera distance, 0.5 m/s is the HITCH rule's scale (it fires at 1 m/s each side). Fix: 'Loop: close the seam' makes the last card a copy of the first |
| BALANCE | with both feet on the floor: the centre of mass (the parts' masses from [de Leva 1996](https://doi.org/10.1016/0021-9290(95)00178-6), each at its capsule's middle) against the support polygon (the hull of the foot capsules' shadow where they touch, within 1 cm) | WARN past 3 cm for 0.1 s or more | the masses are de Leva's (J. Biomech. 29(9), 1223-1230); the 3 cm / 0.1 s bar is MY OWN: a flicker in a fast move is momentum, not a fall. Over the 10 shipped scenes only bo_spin crosses it: its opening stance, 3.8 cm past its feet for 0.28 s (its deepest, 11.5 cm at 1.47 s, lasts 3 frames: momentum, not reported) |
| THROUGH A PROP | only a scene with props (round 38): every body part (fingers aside) and the weapon, each frame, against each prop's boxes (Props.depth: the capsule axis sampled every 1 cm). A part RESTING on a top (lowest surface 0-2 cm above it, or under 5 mm in: Props.rests) is a contact, not a pass. The foot and toe capsules are fitted to the foot's volume and reach 6 cm under the floor at rest, so they are thinned to the sole line grounding stands on (a foot kicked sideways into a box reads only past that line), and a sole may be 1 cm in: the floor itself lets the soles dip 6.75 mm mid-move (the planting pass after grounding, measured) | WARN from 5 mm in (soles 1 cm), FAIL from 2 cm; one finding per run of frames per part and prop | MY OWN bars: 5 mm is the resting allowance (a cushion gives that much), 2 cm is past what any surface gives and reads as passing through at a normal camera distance |
| JUMP | only a scene with an Airborne card (a flight from the take-off card's leave to the landing card's arrival, T s): the height the flight means, g T^2 / 8; the deeper knee's most bend in the 0.5 s before take-off and the 0.25 s after touchdown | WARN when the height is over 0.70 m, the dip under 30 deg or the landing bend under the bar for the fall height (45 deg up to 0.40 m, rising to 77 at 0.59 m, the same line beyond, at most 110) | REAL PEOPLE: h = g T^2 / 8 from flight time (Bosco; Asmussen & Bonde-Petersen); recreational countermovement jump 0.40 +- 0.11 m on a force plate; test dips go to ~90 deg; landing knee flexion soft 117 / stiff 77 deg (DeVita & Skelly 1992, 59 cm drop, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1548984/). Landing vs height: Rosen et al. 2015, 40 cm drop jump, healthy controls peak knee flexion 82.5 +- 9.0 deg, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26665034/; no source gives a clean bend-vs-height line, so the height bar is a straight interpolation between 45 at 0.40 m and DeVita's stiff 77 at 0.59 m, extrapolated beyond. The 0.70 / 30 / 45 bars are mine, set below those |
| CARRY FORM | only a scene where a prop is carried in BOTH hands (Pick up), each frame it is held: the larger upper arm angle from hanging down, both elbows' bend, the hands' middle from the ankles' middle along the floor | WARN for 0.1 s or more of: upper arm over 35 deg out, an elbow under 50 or over 130 deg, hands over 40 cm in front | REAL PEOPLE: a load carried in front rides at knuckle-to-elbow height on bent elbows with the upper arms down (Anderson et al. 2007, anterior load carriage, https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/189155; Snook 1978: 26% more is accepted at knuckle than at elbow height); NIOSH lifting equation H = 25 cm ideal, 25/H falls to 0.63 at 40 cm. The 35 / 50-130 deg bars are mine, set around those postures |
| LIFT FORM | every scene (round 41, #229), each frame: the hands' lowest point against the knees' height, the trunk's flexion from vertical (hips to neck), the deeper knee's bend | WARN for 0.1 s or more of: hands below the knees, trunk over 60 deg, knees under 30 deg (a stoop to the floor); names both angles | REAL PEOPLE: floor lifts squatted peak at 86-115 deg of knee bend ([Saraceni et al. 2021, PMID 34288926](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34288926/): peak knee flexion of the group without / with low back pain: 91.5 / 115.1 deg at lift 1, 85.9 / 95.5 at lift 95, a box off the floor); the lifting studies define a stoop as knees 0-30 deg ([Int J Sports Med 2025, PMID 40368419](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40368419/)); the multifidus quiets as torso flexion goes from 45 to 60 deg ([Shu et al. 2007, PMID 18089925](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18089925/)); stoop lifts load the spine more than squat ones ([Bazrgari et al. 2007, PMID 17103232](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17103232/)). The 60 / 30 deg bars are mine, set from those papers' numbers |
| GAIT | a walk only (round 42, #333): 4+ alternating foot plants (a ball under 5 cm that moves under 0.9 m/s for 4 frames) travelling 0.5 m or more with no flight (both balls off the floor 6 frames = a run, not judged); the step length over the body height (head joint + 14 cm), the cadence, the arm swing peak to peak, the arm's swing against the same-side thigh's | one WARN naming each measure outside: step under 0.30 or over 0.55 of the height, cadence under 75 or over 135 steps/min, arm swing under 10 or over 60 deg, or an arm swinging WITH its own leg (r over 0.3); arms are not judged with a weapon in the hands | REAL PEOPLE: step 0.415 +- 0.038 of height at 1.33 m/s, 21 young adults ([Gait Posture 2009, PMID 19502061](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19502061/)); cadence 105.5 +- 5.2 steps/min at 1.21 m/s ([PMID 29775941](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29775941/)) and 108 +- 7 at 1.4 m/s ([PMID 41879248](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41879248/)); arm swing peak to peak 28.6 / 33.1 +- 11 deg right / left at 4 km/h ([Yamaguchi et al. 2025, PMID 40291287](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40291287/)) and a median 25.3 / 25.7 deg in healthy controls ([PLoS One 2012, PMID 23236504](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23236504/)); arms out of phase with each other and 1:1 with the legs at walking speed ([Kubo et al. 2004, PMID 15351887](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15351887/)). All bars are mine, about 3 SD around those means |
| FLOATS | only a scene with props: the hips' lowest surface over a seat they are over (the hips' middle inside its top) while neither foot is on the ground | WARN 2-15 cm above for 0.1 s or more | MY OWN bars: under 2 cm is resting (Props.REST_GAP); over 15 cm is not "meant to sit" (a jump over a bench); 0.1 s as BALANCE |
| JOINT SPEED | every scene, each frame: a joint's turn relative to the part it hangs from (shoulder, knee, hip, trunk) | WARN over the fastest recorded peak (shoulder 4766, knee 1874, hip 745, trunk 601 deg/s); FAIL over the FAIL bar (6310, 2184, 1117, 901) | the bars are in [docs/research/HUMAN_LIMITS.md](research/HUMAN_LIMITS.md): shoulder from Campbell (ISBS), knee from a kick review's Table 4; hip and trunk FAIL bars are MY 1.5x. Elbow and neck are not graded (no verified number) |
| JOINT TORQUE | every scene: the net moment at the knee and the shoulder (point masses, de Leva shares, rotation left out so a lower bound) | WARN over the recorded strength: knee 184.7 Nm for 75 kg scaled by (mass/75)^(2/3), shoulder 0.70 Nm/kg x mass; FAIL over mean + 2 SD (273 Nm, 1.00 Nm/kg) | [HUMAN_LIMITS.md](research/HUMAN_LIMITS.md): knee from a healthy-adult isokinetic table (excerpt, mixed-age men); shoulder from a handball study (excerpt). The mass scaling is MINE |
| TIPPING | with a foot down: the centre of mass past the support for longer than a fall from its height takes, with no hand or weapon holding | FAIL when the run lasts longer than t = sqrt(2h/g) (about 0.43 s at h 0.9 m) | the free-fall time of the centre of mass, as a bar (MINE; generous: a stumble that is caught is allowed) |

## Which scenes get which rules
- The swing rules (SEQUENCE, HIPS TURN, HEAD QUIET, TIP SPEED, STRIKE FRAMES) only run on strikes in attack scenes:
  not `idle_bat`, `walk_bat`, `dodge_side`, `hit_react` (attack_timing's list), not the get-ups.
- A strike = a tip speed peak at least half the scene's fastest (and over 3 m/s), 0.25 s from any bigger one.

## How the bars were set (and what was thrown out)
- A LURCH rule on raw acceleration was planned and DROPPED after a survey of every scene (`tests/lurch_survey.gd`): a
  jump landing (leap_slam 377 m/s^2) and a get-up (336) are real and scored above combo3's lurch (hips 81, head 247),
  so no acceleration bar separates them. The lurch is caught by HEAD QUIET instead (56 cm in 130 ms).
- HITCH first flagged holds (a held wind-up top is on purpose) and reported one stop three times; both fixed.
- Every rule fires on a series broken on purpose and stays quiet on a good one: `tests/rules_test.gd`.
- FOOT SLIDE, first version (any down frame): 40 findings, every one a landing or a take-off. The settled gate leaves
  one: in `combo3_detail` the right foot creeps 5.4 cm into 'recover' at 3-5 mm a frame (traced frame by frame, real).
- GIMBAL FLIPS (an animator complaint) were MEASURED, not ruled: `tests/flip_survey.gd` reads every joint's turn between
  frames over 32 scenes. Worst 58.4 deg/frame = 3505 deg/s (thrust, the knee); bar = the fastest joint turn on record, a
  pitcher's shoulder internal rotation, 4766 +- 772 deg/s ([Campbell, ISBS, Table 2](https://ojs.ub.uni-konstanz.de/cpa/article/view/1673/1575)),
  mean + 2 SD = 6310 deg/s. Nothing over it, nothing even over the mean; a 180 deg twist planted as a control reads 180 deg/frame and is caught.
  Angles here are swing/twist with joint limits, never Euler gimbals, so flips have no way in; the survey stays as a guard.

## Fix (the button under a finding; `scripts/fixer.gd`)
Nick 10-14: "fixes could update the clip only, not delete them; we always have ctrl+z". A fix changes a card's move
time, its flow flag or its trunk pose; it never adds, deletes, renames or reorders a card (asserted). It is KEPT only
if its own finding is gone and the scene has no more must-fix findings than before; otherwise the cards are put back
byte-identical and the row says why. A kept fix is one Ctrl+Z step (undo now restores cards, not just the body).

| finding | what Fix does |
|---|---|
| TIP SPEED / FIST SPEED | the card's move time x (speed / cap) x 1.05, re-checked, up to 4 times |
| STRIKE FRAMES | the card's move time x 0.85, re-checked, up to 4 times |
| HITCH (no spline) | the card the body stops at becomes a flow card (passed through) |
| HEAD QUIET | the card's spine, neck, head and the hips' slide eased back toward the card before, by the LEAST amount (bisection) that brings the head under 15 cm; the arms ride on the trunk, so the message reports the tip speed change |
| SEQUENCE, HIPS TURN, HITCH in a spline scene | no button: the row says it is in the poses |

Measured on real scenes (`tests/fix_test.gd`): combo3's head fix eases card 10 93% back toward card 9 (56 cm -> under
15), and the tip drops 44.7 -> 31.0 m/s with it. The same fix on the polished combo3 is REFUSED (it would create a
SEQUENCE FAIL and move the lurch to card 26): the 30-card version spreads the lurch over several cards.
