# CH1 reference: the 1949 Levitt "Ranch", Levittown NY (one storey)

Nick's challenge (11-03): a simple American one-storey house, a blueprint that follows engineering rules, a making-of video, a real case for inspiration, done in Kine.

## Source (and what it could and could not give)

- Hofstra University, "The Levitt Home" (Levittown: the birthplace of suburbia): https://omeka.hofstra.edu/exhibits/show/levittown--the-birthplace-of-s/the-levitt-home (the page returned HTTP 403 to my fetch; only the search summary was readable).
- Floor plan image "The Ranch": https://instanthouse.blogspot.com/2011/08/levittown-ny.html (caption: "Notice the unusual entrance through the kitchen"; the page has no dimensions in text).
- History News Network, Levittown: https://www.historynewsnetwork.org/article/43703 (ranch about 800 sq ft, $7,990).

What the readable text confirms: a one-storey Ranch of about 800 sq ft, kitchen at the front with the entrance through it, living room at the rear, two bedrooms and one bath.
What I could NOT read (no dimensioned drawing was reachable as text): the individual room sizes. The overall footprint below is the commonly quoted 32 ft x 25 ft (= 800 sq ft, matches the published area), and the room sizes are MY RECONSTRUCTION that fits that footprint and the published room order. They are the reference the tests hold Kine to; they are not read off the original drawing. Everything not published is standard IRC 2021 practice, marked (IRC).

## Openings (IRC practice, sizes as the Levitt ranch's standard units)

- Entrance door 36 x 80 in (914 x 2032 mm), front wall, in the kitchen, rough opening 2 in wider/taller.
- Interior doors 30 x 80 in (762 x 2032 mm); the kitchen/living passage is a 36 in cased opening.
- Windows: 36 x 48 in (914 x 1219 mm) double-hung, sill 36 in (914 mm) above the floor: kitchen x1 (front), living x2 (rear), bedroom 1 x1 (rear) + x1 (right), bedroom 2 x1 (front) + x1 (right), bath 24 x 36 in x1 (rear).
- Headers: IRC R602.7 table, 2x8 (nominal) doubled for these spans (<= 4 ft), jack (trimmer) studs one per side, king studs one per side, cripples above/below.

## Framing (IRC)

- 2x4 studs (38 x 89 mm actual) at 16 in (406.4 mm) on centre, single bottom plate (pressure treated on slab), double top plate lapped at corners.
- Corners: three studs; partition ends: two studs (backer).
- Ceiling joists 2x8 at 16 in o.c. (spans 3.5 m and 4.0 m to the load-bearing hall/living partition line, IRC R802.4), running front to back.
- Rafters 2x10 (38 x 235 mm) at 24 in (609.6 mm) o.c., ridge board 2x10, collar ties omitted because the ceiling joists are the rafter ties (IRC R802.3).
- Wall sheathing 7/16 in OSB (11 mm); roof sheathing 7/16 in OSB 4x8 sheets; asphalt shingles.
